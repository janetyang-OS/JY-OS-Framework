## FIX Protocol — L6 → L7 Upgrade

FIX-CORE-REASONING

Rule:
Every explanation must include:

1. Cause
2. Immediate effect
3. System-level consequence

AND

1 evidence must be:
- specific (text / data point)
- not general statement

## Transfer Rule

This pattern applies when:

- Student explanations are correct but short
- Evidence is general (not specific)
- Reasoning stops at immediate effect

Apply FIX-CORE-REASONING across:
- Science explanations
- Math data interpretation
- English textual analysis
