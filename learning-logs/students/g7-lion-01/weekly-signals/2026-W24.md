### Gap 1 — Paraphrasing Independence

Status:

→ Week 3 Active

Current condition:

Duke can understand source material and identify important ideas.

However, he still sometimes remains too close to:

* original wording
* original sentence structure
* source logic

Target process:

1. read
2. close source
3. rewrite in own words
4. compare wording
5. compare sentence structure
6. rewrite again if needed

Success criteria:

* wording changed
* structure changed
* meaning preserved

Paraphrasing remains the largest B2 gap.

---

### Gap 2 — Grammar Control in Complex Sentences

Status:

→ Week 4 Active

Current condition:

Simple sentences and some compound sentences are becoming more stable.

However, complex sentences remain unstable when ideas become more advanced.

Common risks:

* long sentence drift
* unclear clause control
* connector confusion
* small grammar errors under idea complexity

Target:

* controlled complexity
* one main idea per sentence
* maximum two clauses per sentence
* clear sentence boundaries
* grammar check before final submission

---

## Redo System Status

Redo remains mandatory.

Current rule:

Understanding information ≠ B2
Repeating information ≠ B2
Explaining information ≠ full B2

Transforming, supporting, evaluating, and defending ideas = B2 direction.

Completion requires:

1. Attempt
2. Gap Identification
3. Redo
4. Final Version

Redo should focus on:

* source wording carryover
* weak evidence
* missing evaluation
* weak explanation
* missing why-it-matters sentence
* unclear implication
* unstable long sentence
* weak final judgement

The goal is not longer writing.

The goal is stronger academic communication quality.
