## Current B2 Gap

Duke’s main gap is now:

→ transforming information into original academic communication

### Gap 1 — Paraphrasing independence

Duke can understand the source and identify key ideas.

However, he still sometimes stays too close to:

* original wording
* original sentence structure
* source logic

Target:

* read
* close source
* rewrite in own words
* check that wording changed
* check that sentence structure changed

### Gap 2 — Supporting evidence

This is the new major gap.

Duke’s main idea is often clear, and his explanation is improving.

However:

* supporting detail is not always enough
* evidence is not always complete
* claims sometimes need stronger proof

Target:

* every major claim needs one evidence sentence
* every major idea needs one why-it-matters sentence
* explanation must connect evidence to meaning

### Gap 3 — Ideas outpace grammar

Duke’s thinking is becoming more mature than his sentence control.

When ideas become more complex, grammar becomes less stable.

Common risks:

* long sentence drift
* connector confusion
* clause control weakness
* small grammar slips

Target:

* controlled complexity
* one main idea per sentence
* maximum two clauses per sentence
* clear sentence boundaries

## Redo System Status

Redo remains mandatory.

Current rule:

Understanding information ≠ B2
Repeating information ≠ B2
Transforming information into original communication = B2

Completion requires:

1. Attempt
2. Gap Identification
3. Redo
4. Final Version

Redo should focus on:

* source wording carryover
* weak evidence
* weak explanation
* missing implication
* unstable long sentence
* unclear academic vocabulary
* weak conclusion

The goal is not to make the answer longer.

The goal is to improve academic communication quality.
