# Case Record｜Continuity Failure Under School Load

Student: G11-Eagle-01
Period: 2026-09 → 2026-10
Case Type: Continuity / Execution Failure
Status: Observation Case

## Background

Eddie previously demonstrated measurable improvement in:

- Sentence Parsing
- Reading Model
- Evidence Use
- Inference Control

However, training volume subsequently declined.

Week 37:
- Formal tasks: 2 / 4
- Reading Exposure: 2 / 5

Week 38:
- Formal tasks: 3 / 4
- Reading Exposure: below target

October:
- Parent requested a complete temporary pause because of school activities and examination preparation.

## Core Observation

The limiting factor shifted from skill acquisition to continuity.

Eddie showed that he could perform the target skills when training occurred.

The system did not obtain enough repeated exposure to determine whether those gains could become durable.

## Failure Pattern

Ability present
→ training dose falls
→ repetition becomes insufficient
→ progression evidence becomes unreliable
→ program pauses completely

## System Learning

This case suggests that a learning system cannot evaluate only:

- skill quality
- task performance
- reasoning accuracy

It must also evaluate:

- minimum viable weekly dose
- continuity under real school pressure
- recovery after interruption
- whether the student can maintain long-horizon learning habits

## Important Distinction

This case does not prove:

- English ability declined
- the student lacks potential
- the training model failed academically

It does demonstrate:

- continuity was not sustained
- the intervention could not accumulate enough repeated evidence
- skill potential did not convert into stable longitudinal progression

## Playbook Implication

Future JY-OS cohorts should track:

1. Skill Progress
2. Execution Consistency
3. Exposure Volume
4. Interruption Risk
5. Re-entry Behavior

A student should not be classified as structurally progressing if skill performance is strong but the minimum viable practice rhythm repeatedly collapses.
