## 3. Decision Threshold Is Not Yet Precise Enough

The next-level skill is not simply:

→ “I would change my mind if more evidence appeared.”

That is too vague.

Duke needs to specify:

→ exactly what evidence would be sufficient to change the decision.

A strong decision threshold should answer:

* what new evidence is required?
* how reliable must it be?
* how strong must its impact be?
* would it change confidence only or change the conclusion?

Classification:

→ DECISION_THRESHOLD_DEVELOPING

---

## 4. Conditional Conclusions Are Emerging

Duke has begun to recognize that a conclusion may need limits.

Examples of stronger structure include:

→ if Y were verified, my conclusion would change.

This is more sophisticated than:

→ absolute judgment.

However, conditional language must become more specific.

Classification:

→ CONDITIONAL_JUDGEMENT_EMERGING
