**Decisive Evidence**

↓

**Explicit Threshold**

↓

↓

### Critical Distinction

A specific threshold is not necessarily a justified threshold.

A complete explanation must establish:

1. What evidence matters most.
2. What condition would change the decision.
3. Why that condition is sufficient.
4. Why weaker evidence would not be sufficient.
5. How the condition affects confidence.
6. Whether the final decision should change.

### Primary Developmental Question

**Why is this amount or quality of evidence enough to act?**
