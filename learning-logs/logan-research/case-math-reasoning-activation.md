# Case Study
Math Reasoning Activation with AI Coach

Student
Logan (Lazy Pig)

Grade
Y9 (IB preparation)

---

## Baseline Observation

Logan demonstrated strong conceptual understanding in mathematics.

IB criteria signals before intervention:

Criterion A (Knowledge)
Level 6

Criterion C/D (Communication / Application)
Level 2–3

Observation:

The student could correctly solve problems but struggled to:

- explain reasoning
- structure written solutions
- communicate mathematical logic clearly.

---

## Intervention

Three short AI coaching sessions were conducted.

Schedule:

Friday  
Saturday  
Sunday  

Duration per session:

Less than 30 minutes.

Total intervention time:

Approximately 90 minutes.

The AI coach focused on prompting the student to:

- explain reasoning steps
- justify mathematical operations
- structure responses clearly.

---

## Outcome

After the three sessions, Logan demonstrated significant improvement in written reasoning.

Observed signal:

Criterion C/D reasoning improved to Level 6.

The student was able to:

- clearly structure explanations
- communicate mathematical reasoning
- justify solution steps.

---

## Interpretation

This case suggests that the improvement was not caused by new mathematical knowledge.

Instead, the intervention activated existing understanding by prompting structured reasoning.

The AI coach functioned primarily as a reasoning scaffold rather than a knowledge instructor.

---

## Research Implication

Short AI-assisted reasoning prompts may help students translate conceptual understanding into structured mathematical explanations.

Further observation is required to determine whether the effect is stable over time.
