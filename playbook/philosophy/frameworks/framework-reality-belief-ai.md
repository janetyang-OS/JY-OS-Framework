# Framework — Reality / Human Belief / AI Answer (3-Layer Model)

## 1-sentence thesis
AI produces answers about the world, but **answers are not reality**; human judgment is the system that connects claims to reality via evidence.

---

## Model (3 Layers)
### Layer 1 — Reality
- What actually is the case.
- Independent of opinions and outputs.

### Layer 2 — Human Belief
- What a person thinks is true.
- Can be accurate, biased, incomplete, or unstable.

### Layer 3 — AI Answer
- A generated proposition (often fluent).
- Can be correct, wrong, or correct-for-the-wrong-reason.

### The key gap
AI answers do not automatically upgrade human belief to knowledge.
The missing bridge is **evidence discipline**.

---

## When to use
Use this framework when:
- students rely on AI for “instant truth”
- parents confuse “more information” with “better judgment”
- discussions require separating confidence from verification

---

## Failure modes
- **FM1: Answer = Reality**  
  “If AI says it, it’s real.”
- **FM2: Belief = Knowledge**  
  “I feel sure, so it’s true.”
- **FM3: Evidence replaced by fluency**  
  “It sounds good” becomes the standard.

---

## Bridge-to-Lab (Reading Judgment)
CAP Daily Loop is the bridge mechanism:

- The selected option is a belief/proposition.
- The evidence sentence is the reality-link attempt.
- Diagnosis codes identify where the bridge fails.
- Fix codes repair the bridge using repeatable steps.

**Operational rule:**  
We do not optimize for more answers.
We optimize for stronger reality-links (evidence → justified judgment).
